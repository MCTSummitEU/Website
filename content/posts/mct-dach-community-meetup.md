![German flag](/images/posts/flag-de.svg) This was a **German-language** local gathering of Microsoft Certified Trainers from the DACH region — not an MCT Summit EU event.

On 4 and 5 September 2026, MCTs met at Microsoft in Munich for a day of sessions, discussion, and networking, with an optional city tour on Saturday. Attendance was free.

- **When:** 4–5 September 2026
- **Where:** [Microsoft](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Microsoft%2C+Walter-Gropius-Stra%C3%9Fe+5%2C+80807+M%C3%BCnchen%2C+Germany), Walter-Gropius-Straße 5, 80807 München, Germany (DE)
- **Organizers:** [Thomas Stensitzki](https://granikos.eu/mct-regional-lead-2024/), [André Thiemann](https://thiemann-solutions.de/)
- **Event site:** [mctgermany.online](https://mctgermany.online/mct-community-meetup/)
- **Programme:** [Sessionize](https://sessionize.com/mct-dach-community-meetup-2026/)

Session slides and demo material, where available, are in our Artifacts repo: [2026-munich-de](https://github.com/MCTSummitEU/Artifacts/tree/main/2026-munich-de).
